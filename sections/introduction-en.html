Introduction back to ToC

Data processing pipelines are a crucial component of any data-centric system today. Machine learning, data integration, and knowledge graph publishing are examples where data processing pipelines are needed. Furthermore, most production systems require data pipelines that support continuous operation and streaming-based capabilities for low-latency computations over large volumes of data. The RDF-Connect Ontology aims to provide a standardized vocabulary for describing these (streaming) data processing pipelines across execution environments, increasing interoperability, reusability, transparency and understanding among different systems and tools. The ontology is designed to be polyglot, supporting various execution environments (e.g., Java, Python, JavaScript, etc.), and provenance-aware (as an extension of the W3C PROV Ontology), enabling tracking of data lineage and transformations throughout the pipeline.
